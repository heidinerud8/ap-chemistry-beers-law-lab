General Chemistry Practice: Density Graphs and Line Equations

Directions: For a mass vs. volume graph, the slope represents density. For a graph of volume in mL vs. volume in cm³, the slope is 1 because 1 cm³ = 1 mL. For linear equations, use y = mx + b, where the slope is the density when y is mass and x is volume.
