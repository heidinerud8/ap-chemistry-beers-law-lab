## Background Information

**Beer's Law** describes the relationship between the concentration of a solution and the amount of light it absorbs:

### A = εbc

Where:
- **A** = Absorbance (unitless, range 0-4)
- **ε** = Molar absorptivity (L·mol⁻¹·cm⁻¹)
- **b** = Path length of light through the solution (cm)
- **c** = Molar concentration of the absorbing species (mol/L)
